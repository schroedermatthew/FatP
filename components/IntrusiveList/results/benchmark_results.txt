Analysis:
- IntrusiveList adds 24 bytes per node (prev + next + owner)
- std::list adds 16 bytes per node PLUS allocator overhead (~8-32 bytes)

- For 1M nodes, IntrusiveList saves ~24-48 MB vs std::list
