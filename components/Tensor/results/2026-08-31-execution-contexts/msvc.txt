Competitors:
  [x] Tensor serial (matched baseline)
  [x] explicit context (default cutoff and forced scheduling)

Design Invariants:
  1. Round-robin execution with randomized order per run
  2. Setup/teardown outside timed regions
  3. All libraries observe same distribution of machine states
  4. Medians are the primary reported statistic
  5. Correctness verified after each benchmark

Workers=1,2,4 (deliberately capped); pool spin=0us plus default-2000us probes.
No affinity or priority override; FATP_BENCH_NO_SCOPE is immaterial to this suite.
Reused inputs; fresh result allocation/init/destruction and scheduling included.
Pool/caller construction excluded; concurrent batches include start/finish barriers.
All output bits checked outside timing. Same kernel and result ownership in every path.
No cold-cache claim. Batch-mean dispersion is NOT per-call p95/p99 latency.
Concurrent rows are inverse throughput per completed call, not individual-call latency.
